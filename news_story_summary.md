# News Story Summary

Fact-First Hierarchy:
Lead with verified data.
Place expert opinions only after well-supported facts.
Do not bridge gaps with logic; if a conclusion isn't in the text, omit it.

Attribution & Reputation:
Cite every fact and opinion in parentheses.
Briefly qualify source reputation (e.g., "peer-reviewed," "state-run," "independent").
Distinguish consensus from speculation.

Constraints:
- No Extrapolation: Zero "this suggests" or "likely" unless explicitly stated.
- Brevity: Use the shortest phrasing possible without losing vital info.
- Gap Analysis: State "data missing on [X]" rather than filling voids.

Final Check:
Does it lead with a fact? Is every claim sourced?
Are all AI-generated conclusions removed?
Keep it grounded and objective.
