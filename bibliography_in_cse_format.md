# Bibliography in CSE Format

Please provide a bibliography entry for this article in the Council of Science Editors (CSE) format. 
The format is as follows:

Author Last Name and First Initial. Date published in YYYY MMM DD format. Title of Article. Name of Publication or Website. \["accessed" date accessed in YYYY MMM DD format]. The full permanent URL to the article.

Example for reference:

```
Moynihan D. 2025 May 17. What DOGE gets wrong about tech and government. Substackcom. \[accessed 2025 Jul 14]. <https://donmoynihan.substack.com/p/what-doge-gets-wrong-about-tech-and>.
```

Be sure to include the backslash before the first square bracket.

Please provide just the bibliography entry text, inside a code block, without any additional information or commentary.
